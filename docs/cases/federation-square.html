04 / SYNTAX

连接：抵达、穿行与停留 Connections: arrival, passage and staying

车站提供到达，文化机构提供目的，公共地面与通廊提供穿行和停留。 The station brings arrival, cultural institutions provide destinations, and public ground and passage enable movement and staying.

抵达 Arrival

车站与电车把城市使用者带到广场边缘。 Station and trams bring city users to the edge.

穿行 Passage

坡地、通廊与街口把CBD和河岸串联。 Slope, passage and crossings connect CBD and river.

停留 Staying

文化入口、边缘座位和活动设施提供不同停留目的。 Cultural entries, seating edges and event infrastructure create different reasons to stay.

车站、街道与河岸在同一片公共地面相接，Atrium补充有遮蔽的通行与停留空间。 Station, streets and river meet across shared civic ground, with Atrium providing covered passage and staying space.

04 / BOUNDARY

边界：建筑与公共地面 Edges: buildings meet civic ground

多栋文化建筑形成非连续但可识别的边界，公共地面把这些界面联成整体。 Several cultural buildings form distinct edges, joined into an ensemble by civic ground.

石材、金属与玻璃形成不同透明程度；入口、外摆和覆盖通廊比表皮图案更直接影响人们如何使用建筑边缘。 Stone, metal and glass provide different transparency, while entries, terraces and covered passage shape edge use more directly than cladding patterns alone.

04 / BOUNDARY

环境：景观、气候与科技 Environment: landscape, climate and technology

墨尔本气候多变；Atrium补充室外广场的遮蔽，数字屏幕与被动环境系统支持日常运营。 Melbourne’s variable conditions make Atrium cover valuable beside the outdoor plaza; digital screens and passive systems support its operation.

景观 Landscape

河岸与较新的Test Garden补充以硬质地面为主的场地；花园于2025年开放。 River frontage and the newer Test Garden complement the paved precinct; the garden opened in 2025.

天气与气候 Weather and climate

温带、天气多变。旧市中心站1月／7月平均最高气温25.9／13.5°C（1855—2014），不代表广场微气候；Atrium提供覆盖条件。 Temperate and variable. The former central station records Jan/Jul mean maxima of 25.9/13.5°C (1855–2014), not plaza microclimate; Atrium provides cover.

科技 Technology

65 m² Digital Façade用于公共内容与活动，配套声学、供电与听力辅助。 A 65 m² Digital Façade supports public content and events with sound, power and hearing support.

04 / CONCLUSION

最突出的三个特点 Three defining qualities

最突出的价值在于地面、覆盖空间与持续运营的协同，而不只是多面体立面。 Its defining strength is the collaboration of ground, covered space and sustained operations, beyond faceted facades alone.

基础设施上的公共地面 Civic ground over infrastructure

通过铁路上盖与坡地，把原本分隔的城市关系重新连接。 Deck and slope reconnect urban relationships separated by rail.

室内外互补 Indoor/outdoor complement

Atrium为露天广场补充覆盖空间与文化入口。 Atrium supplements the outdoor plaza with cover and cultural entrances.

文化与数字活动平台 Cultural and digital platform

文化机构、数字屏幕与全年活动支持社区和游客共同使用。 Institutions, screen and year-round programming support both communities and visitors.
