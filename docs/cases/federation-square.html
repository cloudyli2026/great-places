概况 Overview

01

Federation Square. Federation Square.

不是建筑之间剩下的空地，而是铁路之上被共同建造的城市地面。 A civic ground deliberately built over railway infrastructure, rather than space left between buildings.

人工地面 · 文化集群 · 公共表达 Artificial ground · Cultural ensemble · Public expression

年代 Period 2002年建成；1997年竞赛获胜 Completed 2002; competition won 1997
公布面积 Published area 广场7,500 m²；建筑项目44,000 m² Plaza 7,500 m²; architectural project 44,000 m²
主要用途 Primary uses 文化参观、穿行、免费停留、活动与集会 Culture, passage, informal stay, events and assemblies

Destination visits and through journeys require separate study.

01 / 地面 01 / Ground

广场首先是一项基础设施转换：平台让公共空间跨越铁路；平面不能独自解释这种立体关系。 The deck changes the infrastructural condition. A plan alone cannot explain the resulting vertical relationships.

02 / 边界 02 / Edges

室外广场、Atrium与文化机构入口形成不同开放条件，玻璃透明性与公共可进入性不能直接画等号。 Plaza, Atrium and institutional entrances have different access conditions. Transparency does not establish public access.

03 / 使用 03 / Use

04 / SYNTAX

空间句法 Space syntax

03

先证明哪里能走、哪里能看，再谈整合度。 Establish where people can walk and see before claiming integration.

本章是可检验的方法审计与空间阅读，不是已完成的Angular Integration、Choice或VGA计算。本站没有为04运行并校准正式句法模型，也没有可发布的实测人流。 This chapter audits a testable method and spatial reading. It is not a completed angular integration, choice or VGA calculation. No calibrated formal syntax model or measured pedestrian flow is published for case 04.

一套问题，三种模型 One set of questions, three different models

01 / 城市网络 01 / Urban network

检验车站—CBD—河岸的路径是否经过广场。输入需含真实步行路网、路口过街、开放时段与竖向连接；铁路和机动车道不得当作人行边。 Test whether station–CBD–river journeys pass through the square. Inputs need real pedestrian links, crossings, opening hours and level changes. Rail and traffic lanes cannot become pedestrian edges.

02 / 广场视域 02 / Plaza visibility

检验广场与Atrium入口是否相互可见。输入需分层步行多边形、墙、玻璃、台阶、坡地和临时遮挡。玻璃可见与可穿越要用两个属性。 Test intervisibility of plaza and Atrium entrances. Inputs need level-specific walkable polygons, walls, glass, stairs, slopes and temporary obstructions. Visibility and permeability require separate attributes.

03 / 行为验证 03 / Behavioural validation

区分穿行、等候、自由坐靠、消费与活动观众。按入口、时段和活动状态记录；观察数据与模型分开存档，再检验相关关系。 Separate through movement, waiting, free sitting, consumption and event attendance. Record entrance, time and event state; archive observations separately before testing relationships with models.

04 / BOUNDARY

边界与建筑 Edges & architecture

04

圣马可依靠连续柱廊组织边界；Federation Square依靠多栋建筑、开口与公共通廊共同组织边界。 San Marco organises its edge through continuous arcades; Federation Square combines separate buildings, openings and public passages.

立面图纸与立面照片必须分开 Keep elevation drawings distinct from elevation photographs

尺度章已展示带真实立面投影的城市剖面。下列为原刊标注的立面与城市界面照片，不把摄影写成正投影测绘图。当前未取得可校准的完整四向正立面套图；北侧、河侧、铁路侧及广场内侧按可核实的视点分别阅读。 The scale chapter displays a real urban section with projected elevations. The following are published facade and context photographs, not orthographic surveys. A calibrated four-direction elevation set is unavailable; read the documented street, river, rail and plaza-facing viewpoints separately.

Atrium：建筑内部也是公共空间研究对象 Atrium: building interiors as part of the civic-space study

与圣马可柱廊不同，Atrium不是仅附着在单侧立面上的浅进深边缘，而是可单独进入和穿行的覆盖空间。建筑师资料将其视为联系Flinders Street与河流的公共空间；实际通行时间、活动占用与无障碍路径仍应由现行运营信息或现场核验。 Unlike San Marco’s edge arcade, the Atrium is a covered space that can be entered and traversed in its own right. The architects describe a connection between Flinders Street and the river; current hours, event occupation and step-free continuity still require operational or field checks.

表皮与开口 Cladding and openings

石材、金属与玻璃在照片中形成不同透明程度。表皮图案提供识别，但不能代替入口可见性、可进入性和沿街活动的研究。 Stone, metal and glass present different apparent transparency. Cladding patterns create identity but cannot substitute for entry visibility, access or ground-level activity.

地面与坐靠 Ground and seating edges

坐靠照片显示部分高差被日常使用；这种现象值得记录，但不证明所有台阶满足舒适和无障碍需求。 Photographs show some level changes used as seats. This observation does not establish comfort or accessibility of all steps.

室内与室外 Inside and outside

Atrium提供覆盖条件，室外广场支持更大规模聚集。两者互补的判断需要比较活动布置与普通时段，而非只看建筑空景。 Atrium provides cover while the plaza supports larger gatherings. Their complementarity needs event/non-event comparison rather than empty architectural images alone.

人与建筑的尺度：从图纸回到视点 Human scale: from drawings back to viewpoints

观察顺序：先定位公共地面，再辨认首层开口、坐靠、遮蔽与文化入口，最后查看是否允许不消费的停留。建筑高度、街墙连续率和首层开放比例没有可靠实测来源，暂不填写数字。 Read public ground first, then openings, seating edges, cover and cultural entrances, and finally whether staying requires consumption. No reliable measured heights, edge-continuity ratios or ground-floor openness percentages are available, so no numbers are invented.

04 / LIFE

公共生活 Public life

05

同一片地面：从午间停留，到文化活动、节庆与公共表达。 One ground: everyday pauses, cultural events, festivals and civic expression.

日常使用与活动档案分别阅读。气候不单独设章，遮蔽、雨天路径、日晒与活动时段作为使用条件纳入讨论；本案未开展热舒适或风环境实测。 Read everyday use separately from event archives. Cover, rain routes, sun and timing are conditions of use within this chapter, without a separate climate chapter. No thermal-comfort or wind measurements have been undertaken.

01 / 普通时段：停留、穿行与消费 01 / Everyday: staying, passing and consumption

坐在地面边缘与穿行的照片确认这些使用确实发生，但照片的选择由设计方完成，时间未知，存在展示偏差。应在工作日／周末、午间／傍晚、活动／无活动、室外／Atrium分别采样，区分自由停留与商业座位。 Photographs establish that sitting at edges and walking occur, but designer selection and unknown timing introduce display bias. Sample weekdays/weekends, lunch/evening, event/non-event and outdoor/Atrium conditions separately, distinguishing informal stay from commercial seating.

02 / 文化活动：从舞台到室内公共通廊 02 / Cultural events: screen, stage and covered passage

大屏幕和临时座椅显示可被布置的活动地面；开幕夜Atrium照片由2003年原刊确认。两种场景说明室内外可承载不同活动，不能据此证明全年活动频率、总参与人数或不设门槛的使用权。 Screen and temporary seating show a programmable ground; the 2003 publication identifies the Atrium opening-night scene. The images show different indoor/outdoor uses but cannot establish annual frequency, total attendance or unrestricted access.

03 / 节庆：地面与城市天际线共同成为背景 03 / Festivals: ground and skyline as a shared setting

州图书馆将焰火照片标注为2015年。节庆使广场成为城市集体观看的地点，同时带来临时设施、通行和人群管理问题。这里不从图像估计容量，也不把举办过一次活动解释为目前仍按同样规则运营。 The library dates the fireworks image to 2015. Celebration makes the square a shared viewing place while introducing temporary infrastructure and crowd-management demands. Capacity is not inferred, and a past event does not establish current operating rules.

04 / 公共集会：公共性还包括表达与争论 04 / Assemblies: publicness includes expression and disagreement

2003年和平集会照片确认广场具有公共表达的历史用途。2019年遗产委员会决定进一步提供关于公共空间价值的制度性证据。照片与登记决定相互补充，但都不能替代当前活动许可、安保边界或自主使用规则的核查。 The 2003 peace-rally image documents a civic-expression use. The 2019 heritage determination provides institutional evidence of civic value. These sources complement one another without replacing checks of present permits, security boundaries or informal-use rules.

三条可迁移原则 Three transferable principles

先建立可用的公共地面 Establish usable civic ground

基础设施上盖必须与周边过街、室内通廊和河岸路线一起设计；几何连续不代表公共使用连续。 Design the deck with crossings, covered passages and river routes. Geometric continuity does not automatically provide continuous public use.

让文化入口与自由停留并存 Combine cultural entry and informal stay

公共空间不应只有进馆和消费两种目的；通过座位、边缘和通行路径给自主停留留下位置。 Museum entry and consumption should not be the only purposes. Seating, edges and routes should also accommodate voluntary informal stay.

把运营当作空间条件 Treat operations as a spatial condition

临时围栏、活动搭建和开放时间会改变平面可达性；应将它们纳入验证，而非以建筑照片代替。 Fencing, event installations and hours change access. Include them in verification rather than relying on architecture photographs.
