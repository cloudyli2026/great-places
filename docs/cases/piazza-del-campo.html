03 / INTRO

历史：公共地面与共同规则 History: civic ground and shared rules

广场的整体性既来自地形，也来自跨建筑的市政建设与管理。 Its coherence comes from terrain as well as municipal building and management rules.

地面 Ground

砖铺地与放射石带加强壳形地面的统一性。 Brick paving and radial stone bands reinforce the unity of the shell-like ground.

边界 Edges

市政宫与外围建筑共同围合，不依赖各楼完全等高。 Civic palace and surrounding buildings provide enclosure without every building being equal in height.

公共传统 Civic tradition

Contrade与Palio使广场长期承担社区表达，而非只有观光用途。 Contrade and Palio sustain community expression beyond sightseeing.

03 / SYNTAX

连接：抵达、穿行与停留 Connections: arrival, passage and staying

狭窄入口突然打开为宽阔坡面，是Campo最鲜明的步行体验。 Narrow entrances suddenly opening to the broad slope define Campo’s walking experience.

抵达 Arrival

公共交通到达外围节点，再从街巷进入。 Transit arrives at peripheral nodes, followed by lanes.

穿行 Passage

入口压缩后迅速展开，广场兼有通行与目的地属性。 Compressed entrances open rapidly; the square is both route and destination.

停留 Staying

坡面和边缘让坐、看与交谈有不同位置可选。 Slope and edges provide choices for sitting, viewing and conversation.

这是形态解读；不把出版研究图或照片包装成本网站的空间句法测算。 This is a spatial reading; published drawings and photos are not represented as syntax calculations by this site.

03 / BOUNDARY

边界：建筑与公共地面 Edges: buildings meet civic ground

连续街墙提供整体围合，市政宫与塔楼提供重点；统一不等于完全相同。 Continuous fronts provide enclosure; palace and tower provide focus. Coherence does not require uniformity.

边界包含不同材料、开间与年代的建筑。整体空间秩序来自连续界面与共同地面，不是把每栋建筑画成同一种立面。 Edges include different materials, bays and periods. Continuous fronts and shared ground create coherence, not identical individual facades.

03 / BOUNDARY

环境：景观、气候与科技 Environment: landscape, climate and technology

内陆丘陵气候、开敞砖地与建筑阴影决定使用条件；树木不是核心广场的主要围合元素。 Inland hill climate, exposed brick ground and building shade shape use; trees are not the core square’s enclosing device.

景观 Landscape

坡地和Fonte Gaia提供地形与水的体验，核心不是绿地公园。 Slope and Fonte Gaia provide terrain and water experiences; the core is not a planted park.

天气与气候 Weather and climate

夏季较热，冬季较凉，秋季降雨较多。LaMMA的1961—1990基准年降雨785.7 mm；建筑阴影提供局部缓冲，雨会影响赛事。 Hotter summers, cooler winters and wetter autumns. LaMMA baseline rainfall is 785.7 mm/year (1961–1990); building shade offers local relief and rain affects racing.

科技 Technology

以信息、交通和赛事安全管理为主，不把历史空间误写成数字展示广场。 Information, transit and event management matter more than digital spectacle.

03 / LIFE

平日：人们怎样使用这里 Ordinary days: how people use the place

普通日的坡面是穿行、短坐、交谈与观看建筑的共同地面。 On ordinary days, the slope supports passage, short stays, conversation and views of architecture.

普通日可以慢行、坐卧与观看；餐饮消费集中在边缘。这里的开放并不意味着所有位置都适合所有身体条件。 Ordinary days allow walking, sitting and viewing, with dining at the edges. Openness does not make every position equally accessible.

03 / LIFE

节假日与活动：地面的另一种状态 Holidays and events: the ground transforms

Palio不是普通周末的放大版：赛道、围栏、看台和入口控制改变了整个使用方式。 Palio is not simply a busier weekend: track, barriers, grandstands and controlled entries change how the ground works.

03 / CONCLUSION

最突出的三个特点 Three defining qualities

最鲜明的价值在于坡地、街巷与社区仪式的共同作用，而不只是壳形轮廓。 Its defining value lies in slope, lanes and civic ritual acting together, beyond the shell-shaped outline.

可坐卧的坡地 An inhabitable slope

地面本身就参与停留与观看，而不只是一片铺装。 Ground itself supports staying and viewing rather than acting only as paving.

街巷收束与展开 Compression and release

狭窄街巷在进入广场时突然开敞，增强整体空间辨识。 Narrow lanes release into the square, strengthening its spatial identity.

社区仪式的延续 Continuity of civic ritual

Palio将地方社区和公共地面持续联系起来。 Palio keeps local communities connected to civic ground.

主要限制：坡地不等于普遍无障碍，夏季遮阴有限，赛事时自由通行受到明显限制。 Principal limitations: slope is not universal accessibility, summer shade is limited, and events restrict free passage.
