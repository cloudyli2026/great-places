# San Marco — verified dimensions and drawing inventory

## Published geometric dimensions (NOT a site survey)
Nijhuis and Wiers, *Exploring the Visual*, p.121, describe Piazza San Marco as a trapezoid **175 m long**, **81 m wide at the Campanile end**, and **56 m wide near Palazzo Reale**. Source: https://unglueit-files.s3.amazonaws.com/ebf/981b431e3d4b424398f37cad8d1bafd3.pdf

These are published approximate geometric descriptions, **not a certified cadastral or topographic survey**. They are not the dimensions of the entire Piazza + Piazzetta ensemble. Do not derive a precise area from them; the real boundaries and end conditions are irregular. As a conceptual trapezoid only, (81+56)/2 ×175 = 11,987.5 m²; this is **not** an authoritative area figure.

## Do not confuse
- Drawing page size with physical scale.
- Historic tower dimensions with post-1902 reconstruction.
- Perspective paintings with metric survey.
- Public-domain archival images with copyright-protected contemporary architectural PDFs.
