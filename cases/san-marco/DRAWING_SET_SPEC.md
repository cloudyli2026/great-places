# San Marco — five-plate drawing set

## 01 — Piazza physical plan (target 1:1000)
Status: source gathering. Separate Piazza, Piazzetta and Molo. Record north, CRS, scale bar, survey date and building footprint provenance. Published ~175m length and ~81/56m widths are independent cross-checks only, not georeferencing control points.

## 02 — Cross-Piazza section A–A (target 1:200)
Status: pending scale extraction. Cut from Procuratie Vecchie across open Piazza to Procuratie Nuove. Show portico clear height, pavement and cornice only where measured evidence exists. Source candidate: https://www.archilovers.com/projects/253531/renovation-of-the-historic-procuratie-vecchie-building.html . Do not mirror the Vecchie drawing to invent Nuove geometry.

## 03 — Piazzetta to Molo section B–B (target 1:200)
Status: awaiting dated waterfront survey. Show paving, steps, lagoon edge and water datum. Do not infer quay elevations from perspective photos.

## 04 — Ordinary-day occupancy plan (target 1:1000)
Status: awaiting municipal concession footprints and dated observation. Differentiate free lingering, paid café seating, movement, service and passage clearances. A public-market stall plan is not a café terrace permit plan.

## 05 — Festival conversion plan (target 1:1000)
Status: event ordinance located; spatial geometry pending. Choose one dated Redentore or Carnival event. Map its verified crowd sectors, barriers, entrances, emergency routes and suspended occupations. Do not combine years.

## Metadata required on every plate
Plate ID; creator; date; source URL; source type; geometric accuracy; scale basis; coordinate reference system; reuse rights; observation date; uncertainty.

## Verification gates
- Geometry: source scale and dimensions readable, georeferencing residual documented.
- Sections: independently sourced building heights and vertical datum.
- Human use: dated observation or authorized concession plan.
- Publication: clear distinction between facts, approximate values and hypotheses.
