# San Marco — Public ground, café terraces and event conversion

Research update: 2026-11-07. Sources below are linked to municipal originals. This is an evidence inventory, not an on-site occupancy survey.

## 1. Three different legal/spatial layers

**A. Permanent public ground**: Piazza, Piazzetta, porticoes, approaches and lagoon waterfront. Public accessibility must not be equated with unlimited occupation.

**B. Concession-based café terraces (plateatici)**: licensed occupation of public or publicly accessible ground. Municipal service explicitly administers these permits. Exact terrace polygons, operator, authorized furniture counts and term dates are **not yet obtained**. [Municipal plateatici service](https://www.comune.venezia.it/it/content/occupazione-suolo-plateatici).

**C. Temporary event occupation**: stages, queueing, restricted sectors, emergency corridors, security closures, temporary suspension of concessions. These should be mapped with their event date, not as permanent features.

## 3. Quantitative measures to calculate *after* permits and measured plans are acquired

- **Open ground ratio** = accessible open ground / defined study area. Explicitly state whether porticoes are counted.
- **Licensed occupation ratio** = sum of valid licensed terrace polygons / accessible ground in the same study boundary. Avoid double-counting market stalls and terraces.
- **Free sitting provision** = count/length of verified publicly accessible seating and edges; differentiate informal sitting from officially permitted sitting.
- **Event conversion ratio** = event-controlled ground / ordinary-day freely accessible ground for that dated event.
- **Pedestrian clear width** = verified unobstructed passage width at pinch points under ordinary and event configurations.

All numerators and denominators remain **null** until a traceable plan and date-specific permit records are obtained. No crowd-capacity or dwell-time estimate should be inferred from a photograph.

## 4. Research plan for café seating

1. Locate café terraces (e.g. historic cafés) as **candidate points only**, not inferred polygon footprints.
2. Request or locate current **plateatico concession drawings / planimetrie** through municipal records or the operator; confirm their effective dates.
3. Georeference each approved polygon onto the same contemporary base map as portico entrances and street routes.
4. Conduct photographic verification in at least two seasons, with timestamp and source/rights recorded.
5. Analyze edges: paid seating → public passage → open ceremonial ground. Record transitions, barriers, table spacing and service circulation only when measurable.

## 5. Event and ordinary-day paired diagrams

Produce two plans at the **same extent and scale**:
- **Ordinary mode:** café terraces, through-routes, free staying, entrances, service flows.
- **Redentore event mode:** verified sector boundaries, entrance gates, barriers, evacuation corridors and any suspended occupations, **using one specific year's official plan**.

Do not merge different years' festival arrangements into one fictional event plan.

## 6. Architectural inference to test

Hypothesis: a relatively unobstructed central ground plus inhabited edges allows both everyday activity and ceremonial reconfiguration. Alternative explanation: regulatory control and event management—not geometry alone—enable this flexibility. The comparison must separate **form**, **rights of occupation**, and **temporary operations**.
